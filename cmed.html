CMED Health LTD

Transforming Healthcare with Technology

Introduction

CMED Health Limited, a health-tech private company registered under Companies Act 1994 (c-132867/2016, dated 04-09-2016) that is transforming the healthcare landscape using its end-to-end comprehensive multi-layered scalable digital Healthcare platform by connecting people with health services, early diagnosis, health education & referrals for intervention to achieve Health Inclusion (UHC) for SDG. The business which also includes Digital Healthcare Solution Design & Development, AI driven Health Risk Assessment and Intervention System Design & Development, Digital Preventive & Primary Healthcare Services, Digital Community Healthcare Services, Smart Health Monitoring Devices & Consumables sale, Health Education & Promotive Care Services, Data Collection & Data Analysis Tool Development, Telemedicine Services, IT Enabled Services, Technical & Operational Support and Training.

At A Glance

CCMED Health is a health-tech startup in Bangladesh transforming healthcare with its end-to-end multi-layered scalable smart health platform 'Susastho,' (https://bit.ly/3BW4H9K) serving millions of lives to reduce health risks, cost, and time and achieve Universal Health Coverage. Leveraging 4IR technologies, CMED democratizes access to health education, screening, diagnosis & intervention in the community with a sustainable pathway to care while reducing the digital divide. Emphasizing responsible & equitable uses of AI, our vision is to create a world where everyone has access to affordable quality healthcare. CMED established comprehensive patient-centered healthcare service through Digital General Practitioner models for Urban & Rural populations as well as workforces in corporate offices and factories. Susastho platform navigates users to the next level of resources to ensure the continuum of care and captures every transaction of health data to build AI-based risk prediction for individuals and identify populations' health concerns towards establishing personalized health insurance. CMED’s initiative, SuSastho.AI is an AI solution that aims to address Sexual, Reproductive, and Mental Health challenges by overcoming societal taboos and limited access to information and care. Susastho.AI involves an open-source multilingual Generative AI engine that provides health education, screening, risk assessment, referral, doctor consultation, and data analytics. CMED’s goal is to expand the focus to other health issues and scale up to similar socio-economic regions through clinical, cultural, and user-centric evaluations. Till now, Susastho platform has impacted 4 million people and empowered 3000+ female health workers to provide comprehensive healthcare in communities.
